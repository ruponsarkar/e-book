## Preface

Assam-specific questions decide ranks in the state's recruitment exams. In the Assam Police Constable written test, 20 of 100 questions are on Assam's history, geography, polity and economy. In the Sub-Inspector paper, 35 of 100 are on the history and culture of Assam and India. ADRE Grade III and IV papers also test Assam within Social Studies and GK.

This book covers that ground in 24 chapters and about 1,830 questions. It favours accuracy over volume: every answer is traced to a source, and no question is passed off as a previous-year question unless it comes from a real paper.

## How to use this book

1. **Read the revision notes first.** Each chapter opens with short notes and a key-facts box. Use them for a quick revision the week before the exam.
2. **Practise with the question bank, then test yourself. In the chapter banks, the answer and a short explanation follow each question. For timed practice, use the five Test Papers, which give the answer key only at the end.**
3. **Read every explanation, including those for questions you got right.** Explanations add related facts that often turn up as separate questions.
4. **Filter by your exam.** Every question carries exam tags. Use them to focus on what your exam actually tests.

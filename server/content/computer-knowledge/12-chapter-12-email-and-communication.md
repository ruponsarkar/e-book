## 12.1 Revision notes / পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

- **Email** (electronic mail) sends messages over the internet. **Ray Tomlinson** sent the first network email in **1971** and chose the **@** sign.\
১৯৭১ত ৰে টমলিনছনে প্ৰথম নেটৱৰ্ক ইমেইল পঠায় আৰু **@** চিন বাছি লয়।
- Address format: **username@domain**, e.g. `ramen.das@gmail.com`.
- **To** (main receiver), **CC** (carbon copy — others see these addresses), **BCC** (blind carbon copy — hidden from other receivers).\
**BCC**-ত থকা ঠিকনা আন প্ৰাপকে দেখা নাপায়।
- **Protocols:** **SMTP** sends mail; **POP3** downloads mail to one device; **IMAP** keeps mail in sync on the server.
- **Folders:** Inbox, Sent, Drafts (unsent), Outbox (waiting to be sent), Spam/Junk (unwanted bulk mail), Trash.
- **Attachment** = a file sent with an email (paper-clip icon). **Reply** goes to the sender; **Reply All** to everyone; **Forward** sends to a new person.
- Other tools: instant messaging, video conferencing (e.g. Google Meet, Microsoft Teams), blogs, social media.

## 16.1 Revision notes / পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

| Initiative / উদ্যোগ | Key facts / মূল তথ্য |
| --- | --- |
| **Digital India** | Launched **1 July 2015**; aims at digital infrastructure, services on demand and digital literacy |
| **Aadhaar** | **12-digit** identity number issued by **UIDAI** |

| **e-Sanjeevani** | National telemedicine service |

**Assam / অসম**
- **Sewa Setu** (sewasetu.assam.gov.in) is Assam's online portal for citizen services under the **Assam Right to Public Services (ARTPS) Act, 2012**; developed by **NIC Assam**, it lists over **800** services (portal, checked 19 November 2026).\
**চেৱা সেতু** অসমৰ নাগৰিক সেৱাৰ অনলাইন প'ৰ্টেল; ARTPS আইন, ২০১২ৰ অধীনত; NIC অসমে বিকশিত কৰিছে।
- **e-Khazana** (state treasury payments) became available on Sewa Setu after its launch on 1 July 2024.

## 2.1 Rules / নিয়ম

**Kinds of nouns / বিশেষ্যৰ প্ৰকাৰ:** proper (Brahmaputra), common (river), collective (herd), material (gold), abstract (bravery). Nouns are also **countable** (book – books) or **uncountable** (information, furniture, advice, luggage, scenery).\
নামবাচক (Brahmaputra), জাতিবাচক (river), সমষ্টিবাচক (herd), দ্ৰব্যবাচক (gold), ভাৱবাচক (bravery)। বিশেষ্য **গণনীয়** (book – books) বা **অগণনীয়** (information, furniture, advice, luggage, scenery) হ'ব পাৰে।

**Uncountable nouns never take -s or a/an:** *some information, a piece of advice, pieces of furniture*.\
**অগণনীয় বিশেষ্যত -s বা a/an নবহে:** *some information, a piece of advice, pieces of furniture*।

| Plural rule / বহুবচনৰ নিয়ম | Examples / উদাহৰণ |
| --- | --- |
| Irregular / অনিয়মিত | child – children, ox – oxen, mouse – mice, tooth – teeth |
| -f/-fe → -ves | knife – knives, leaf – leaves (but roof – roofs, chief – chiefs) |
| -o → -es | potato – potatoes, hero – heroes (but photo – photos) |
| -is → -es | analysis – analyses, crisis – crises |
| -on → -a | criterion – criteria, phenomenon – phenomena |
| Compound: main word plural / যৌগিক: মূল শব্দ বহুবচন | sons-in-law, passers-by |
| Same form / একে ৰূপ | sheep, deer |

**Pronoun case / সৰ্বনামৰ ৰূপ:** subject (I, he, she, we, they, who) — object (me, him, her, us, them, whom) — possessive (mine, his, hers, ours, theirs, whose).\
কৰ্তা ৰূপ — কৰ্ম ৰূপ — সম্বন্ধবাচক ৰূপ।
- After a preposition or *let*, use the object form: *between you and me*, *let him go*.\
সম্বন্ধসূচক অব্যয় বা *let*-ৰ পিছত কৰ্ম ৰূপ বহে।
- *Each, every, one* are singular: *each … his*, *one … one's*.\
*Each, every, one* একবচন।
- *Its* = possessive; *it's* = it is.\
*Its* সম্বন্ধবাচক; *it's* = it is।

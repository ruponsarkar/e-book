## 4.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Basic rule / মূল নিয়ম:** a singular subject takes a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb. *The boy plays. The boys play.*\
একবচন কৰ্তাৰ পিছত একবচন ক্ৰিয়া, বহুবচন কৰ্তাৰ পিছত বহুবচন ক্ৰিয়া বহে।

| Rule / নিয়ম | Correct example / শুদ্ধ উদাহৰণ |
| --- | --- |
| Two subjects joined by **and** → plural | Ram and Hari **are** friends. |
| But one idea or one person → singular | Bread and butter **is** my breakfast. The poet and singer **has** come. |
| **Each, every, either, neither, everyone, nobody** → singular | Each of the boys **has** a pen. |
| **Either … or / neither … nor / not only … but also** → verb agrees with the nearer subject | Neither the teacher nor the students **were** present. |
| **as well as, along with, with, together with** → verb agrees with the first subject | The captain, as well as the players, **is** happy. |
| **The number of** → singular; **a number of** → plural | The number of cars **is** rising. A number of cars **are** parked. |
| **One of + plural noun** → singular verb | One of my friends **lives** in Jorhat. |
| Distance, money, time as one amount → singular | Ten kilometres **is** a long walk. |
| Nouns plural in form but singular in meaning (news, mathematics, physics) → singular | The news **is** good. |
| Always plural: scissors, trousers, spectacles, cattle, police | The police **have** arrived. |
| **Many a + singular noun** → singular | Many a student **has** failed. |
| **More than one + singular noun** → singular | More than one boy **was** absent. |

**Remember:** a phrase between the subject and the verb does not change the subject: *The box of apples **is** heavy.*\
**মনত ৰাখিব:** কৰ্তা আৰু ক্ৰিয়াৰ মাজৰ বাক্যাংশই কৰ্তা সলনি নকৰে।

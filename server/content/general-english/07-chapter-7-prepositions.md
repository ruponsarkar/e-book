## 7.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Time / সময়:** *at* + exact time (*at 5 o'clock, at night*); *on* + day/date (*on Monday, on 15 August*); *in* + month/year/season/part of day (*in July, in 2026, in the morning*).\
নিৰ্দিষ্ট সময়ত *at*; বাৰ আৰু তাৰিখত *on*; মাহ, বছৰ, ঋতুত *in*।

**Place / স্থান:** *at* a point (*at the gate*); *in* inside an area (*in Assam, in the room*); *on* a surface (*on the table*).\
বিন্দুত *at*; ভিতৰত *in*; পৃষ্ঠত *on*।

| Pair / যোৰ | Use / ব্যৱহাৰ |
| --- | --- |
| since / for | *since* + point of time; *for* + period |
| between / among | *between* two; *among* more than two |
| beside / besides | *beside* = next to; *besides* = in addition to |
| in / into | *in* = position; *into* = movement inside |
| by / with | *by* the doer or means; *with* the instrument: *killed by a hunter with a gun* |

**Fixed prepositions (common in exams) / নিৰ্দিষ্ট প্ৰিপজিচন:** *afraid of, angry with (a person), angry at (a thing), good at, fond of, interested in, married to, die of (disease), suffer from, comply with, abide by, accused of, charged with, congratulate on, prevent from, insist on, aware of, consist of, independent of, dependent on, different from, according to.*

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\
*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 8.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Kinds of conjunctions / সংযোজকৰ প্ৰকাৰ**

| Kind / প্ৰকাৰ | Examples / উদাহৰণ |
| --- | --- |
| Coordinating (FANBOYS) / সমতুল্য | for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so |
| Subordinating / অধীনস্থ | because, although, though, if, unless, until, when, while, since, as, that, lest |
| Correlative (pairs) / যোৰ সংযোজক | either … or, neither … nor, both … and, not only … but also, whether … or, no sooner … than, hardly … when, so … that, such … that |

- Do not use two conjunctions for one link: ~~Although he is poor, but he is honest~~ → *Although he is poor, he is honest*.\
একেটা সংযোগৰ বাবে দুটা সংযোজক নিদিব।
- **Unless** = if not; do not add *not* again: *Unless you work hard, you will fail*.\
**Unless** মানেই if not; পুনৰ *not* নিদিব।
- **Lest** is followed by **should**: *Walk carefully lest you should fall*.\
**Lest**-ৰ পিছত **should** বহে।
- **Until** already means "up to the time": ~~until … not~~ is wrong in most sentences.\
- **Kinds of clauses / উপবাক্যৰ প্ৰকাৰ:** noun clause (*What he said* is true), adjective/relative clause (the man *who came yesterday*), adverb clause (*When it rained*, we stayed in).\
বিশেষ্য, বিশেষণ আৰু ক্ৰিয়া-বিশেষণ উপবাক্য।
- **Relative pronouns:** *who* (persons, subject), *whom* (persons, object), *whose* (possession), *which* (things), *that* (persons or things, defining clauses).\

### A. Choosing the conjunction / সংযোজক বাছনি

**1.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **He is poor ___ honest.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) and\
(B) so\
(C) but\
(D) because\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) but**\
Explanation: Rule: 'But' shows contrast between 'poor' and 'honest'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'But'-এ 'poor' আৰু 'honest'-ৰ মাজৰ বৈপৰীত্য দেখুৱায়।\
*B11-C08-0001 · Coordinating conjunctions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**2.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **I stayed at home ___ it was raining.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) although\
(B) because\
(C) unless\
(D) but\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) because**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Because' gives the reason.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Because'-এ কাৰণ দেখুৱায়।\
*B11-C08-0002 · Subordinating conjunctions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**3.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **___ he worked hard, he failed.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Because\
(B) Unless\
(C) Since\
(D) Although\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Although**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Although' shows a surprising contrast.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Although'-এ অপ্ৰত্যাশিত বৈপৰীত্য দেখুৱায়।\
*B11-C08-0003 · Subordinating conjunctions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**4.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **You will not pass ___ you study regularly.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) if\
(B) because\
(C) unless\
(D) although\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) unless**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Unless' means 'if not'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Unless' মানে 'if not'।\
*B11-C08-0004 · Unless · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**5.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **Walk carefully lest you ___ fall.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) will\
(B) should\
(C) would not\
(D) may not\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) should**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Lest' is followed by 'should' + base verb.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Lest'-ৰ পিছত 'should' + মূল ক্ৰিয়া বহে।\
*B11-C08-0005 · Lest · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

**6.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **No sooner had he reached the station ___ the train left.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) than\
(B) when\
(C) then\
(D) that\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) than**\
Explanation: Rule: The fixed pair is 'no sooner … than'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: নিৰ্দিষ্ট যোৰ 'no sooner … than'।\

**7.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **Hardly had I left home ___ it started to rain.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) than\
(B) then\
(C) when\
(D) as\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) when**\
Explanation: Rule: The fixed pair is 'hardly … when' (also 'scarcely … when').\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: নিৰ্দিষ্ট যোৰ 'hardly … when'।\

**8.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **He is not only intelligent ___ hard-working.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) and also\
(B) but also\
(C) as well\
(D) but\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) but also**\
Explanation: Rule: The pair is 'not only … but also'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: যোৰটো 'not only … but also'।\

**9.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **The box was so heavy ___ I could not lift it.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) than\
(B) as\
(C) which\
(D) that\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) that**\
Explanation: Rule: 'So … that' shows result.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: ফল দেখুৱাবলৈ 'so … that'।\
*B11-C08-0009 · Correlative conjunctions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**10.** Choose the grammatically correct sentence:\
ব্যাকৰণগতভাৱে শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Although he is rich, but he is not happy.\
(B) Although he is rich, yet but he is not happy.\
(C) Although he is rich, so he is not happy.\
(D) Although he is rich, he is not happy.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Although he is rich, he is not happy.**\
Explanation: Rule: Do not use 'but' after 'although'; one conjunction is enough.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'although'-ৰ পিছত 'but' নিদিব; এটা সংযোজকেই যথেষ্ট।\
*B11-C08-0010 · Double conjunctions · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**11.** Choose the grammatically correct sentence:\
ব্যাকৰণগতভাৱে শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus.\
(B) Unless you do not hurry, you will miss the bus.\
(C) Unless you not hurry, you will miss the bus.\
(D) Unless if you hurry, you will miss the bus.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Unless' is already negative; adding 'not' is wrong.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Unless' নিজেই নেতিবাচক; 'not' যোগ কৰা ভুল।\
*B11-C08-0011 · Unless · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

### B. Clauses and relative pronouns / উপবাক্য আৰু সম্বন্ধবাচক সৰ্বনাম

**12.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **This is the boy ___ won the prize.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) whom\
(B) who\
(C) which\
(D) whose\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) who**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Who' refers to a person and is the subject of 'won'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Who'-এ মানুহক বুজায় আৰু 'won'-ৰ কৰ্তা।\
*B11-C08-0012 · Relative pronouns · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**13.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **The man ___ you met yesterday is my uncle.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) which\
(B) whose\
(C) whom\
(D) what\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) whom**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Whom' is the object of 'met'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Whom' হৈছে 'met'-ৰ কৰ্ম।\

খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) who\
(B) whom\
(C) which\
(D) whose\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) whose**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Whose' shows possession.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Whose'-এ অধিকাৰ বুজায়।\

**15.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **The book ___ I bought is interesting.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) which\
(B) who\
(C) whom\
(D) whose\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) which**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Which' (or 'that') refers to things.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: বস্তু বুজাবলৈ 'which' (বা 'that')।\
*B11-C08-0015 · Relative pronouns · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**16.** In 'I know that he is honest', the part 'that he is honest' is a:\
'I know that he is honest' বাক্যত 'that he is honest' অংশটো কি?\
(A) Adjective clause\
(B) Adverb clause\
(C) Phrase\
(D) Noun clause\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Noun clause**\
Explanation: Rule: It is the object of 'know', so it does the work of a noun.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: ই 'know'-ৰ কৰ্ম, সেয়ে বিশেষ্যৰ কাম কৰে।\
*B11-C08-0016 · Kinds of clauses · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

**17.** In 'The house where I was born is old', the part 'where I was born' is a:\
'The house where I was born is old' বাক্যত 'where I was born' অংশটো কি?\
(A) Noun clause\
(B) Adverb clause\
(C) Adjective clause\
(D) Main clause\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) Adjective clause**\
Explanation: Rule: It describes the noun 'house'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: ই বিশেষ্য 'house'-ৰ বৰ্ণনা দিয়ে।\
*B11-C08-0017 · Kinds of clauses · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

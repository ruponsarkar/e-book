# Chapter 9 — Active and Passive Voice / অধ্যায় ৯ — কৰ্তৃবাচ্য আৰু কৰ্মবাচ্য

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\
*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 9.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Rule / নিয়ম:** object of the active sentence → subject of the passive; verb → *be* + past participle (V3); subject → *by* + object form.\
কৰ্তৃবাচ্যৰ কৰ্ম কৰ্মবাচ্যৰ কৰ্তা হয়; ক্ৰিয়া → *be* + V3; কৰ্তা → *by* + কৰ্ম ৰূপ।

| Tense / কাল | Active / কৰ্তৃবাচ্য | Passive / কৰ্মবাচ্য |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple present | He writes a letter. | A letter **is written** by him. |
| Present continuous | He is writing a letter. | A letter **is being written** by him. |
| Present perfect | He has written a letter. | A letter **has been written** by him. |
| Simple past | He wrote a letter. | A letter **was written** by him. |
| Past continuous | He was writing a letter. | A letter **was being written** by him. |
| Past perfect | He had written a letter. | A letter **had been written** by him. |
| Simple future | He will write a letter. | A letter **will be written** by him. |
| Modal | He can write a letter. | A letter **can be written** by him. |

- Perfect continuous tenses and the future continuous are normally not changed into the passive.\
পুৰা চলিত কাল আৰু চলিত ভৱিষ্যৎ সাধাৰণতে কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলনি কৰা নহয়।
- **Imperative:** *Open the door.* → *Let the door be opened.* / *Please help me.* → *You are requested to help me.*\
আদেশসূচক বাক্য: *Let … be + V3* বা *You are requested to …*।
- **Questions:** *Who wrote this?* → *By whom was this written?*; *Did he break it?* → *Was it broken by him?*\
- Some verbs take other prepositions: *known to, pleased with, surprised at, filled with, interested in*.\

### A. Active to passive / কৰ্তৃবাচ্যৰ পৰা কৰ্মবাচ্য

**1.** Choose the correct passive voice form of: **She sings a song.**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) A song is sung by her.\
(B) A song was sung by her.\
(C) A song is being sung by her.\
(D) A song has been sung by her.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) A song is sung by her.**\
Explanation: Rule: Simple present: is/am/are + V3.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: সাধাৰণ বৰ্তমান: is/am/are + V3।\
*B11-C09-0001 · Simple present · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**2.** Choose the correct passive voice form of: **The boy broke the window.**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) The window is broken by the boy.\
(B) The window was broken by the boy.\
(C) The window has been broken by the boy.\
(D) The window was break by the boy.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) The window was broken by the boy.**\
Explanation: Rule: Simple past: was/were + V3 (broken).\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: সাধাৰণ অতীত: was/were + V3 (broken)।\
*B11-C09-0002 · Simple past · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

(B) A bridge is built by them.\
(C) A bridge was being built by them.\
(D) A bridge has been built by them.\

Explanation: Rule: Present continuous: is/are being + V3.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: চলিত বৰ্তমান: is/are being + V3।\

**10.** Choose the correct passive voice form of: **Who wrote this poem?**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\

ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Who' → 'By whom', আৰু প্ৰশ্নৰ ক্ৰম (was + কৰ্তা) থাকে।\

(C) Did the food cooked by her?\
(D) Has the food been cooked by her?\

ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Did' + মূল ক্ৰিয়া → 'was/were' + V3।\

### B. Passive to active / কৰ্মবাচ্যৰ পৰা কৰ্তৃবাচ্য

**13.** Choose the correct active voice form of: **The letter was posted by Rina.**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Rina posts the letter.\
(B) Rina posted the letter.\
(C) Rina had posted the letter.\
(D) Rina has posted the letter.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) Rina posted the letter.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Was posted' is simple past, so the active is 'posted'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Was posted' সাধাৰণ অতীত, সেয়ে কৰ্তৃবাচ্যত 'posted'।\
*B11-C09-0013 · Passive to active · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**14.** Choose the correct active voice form of: **The match will be won by our team.**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Our team would win the match.\
(B) Our team wins the match.\
(C) Our team will be winning the match.\
(D) Our team will win the match.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Our team will win the match.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Will be won' → 'will win'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Will be won' → 'will win'।\
*B11-C09-0014 · Passive to active · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**15.** Choose the correct active voice form of: **The house is being painted by the workers.**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) The workers were painting the house.\
(B) The workers paint the house.\
(C) The workers are painting the house.\
(D) The workers have painted the house.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) The workers are painting the house.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Is being + V3' → present continuous 'are painting'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Is being + V3' → চলিত বৰ্তমান 'are painting'।\
*B11-C09-0015 · Passive to active · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

# Chapter 10 — Direct and Indirect Speech / অধ্যায় ১০ — প্ৰত্যক্ষ আৰু পৰোক্ষ উক্তি

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\
*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 10.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Tense change when the reporting verb is in the past / ৰিপ'ৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়া অতীত কালত থাকিলে কালৰ পৰিৱৰ্তন**

| Direct / প্ৰত্যক্ষ | Indirect / পৰোক্ষ |
| --- | --- |
| simple present (write) | simple past (wrote) |
| present continuous (is writing) | past continuous (was writing) |
| present perfect (has written) | past perfect (had written) |
| simple past (wrote) | past perfect (had written) |
| will / can / may | would / could / might |

- No tense change if the reporting verb is in the present (*He says, "I am ill."* → *He says that he is ill.*) or the statement is a universal truth (*The teacher said that the earth moves round the sun*).\
ৰিপ'ৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়া বৰ্তমান কালত থাকিলে বা চিৰন্তন সত্য হ'লে কাল সলনি নহয়।

| Word / শব্দ | Changes to / সলনি হয় |
| --- | --- |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the previous day / the day before |
| here / this | there / that |
| ago | before |

- **Questions:** *said to* → *asked*; *wh*-word or *if/whether*; statement word order: *He said to me, "Where do you live?"* → *He asked me where I lived.*\
প্ৰশ্নত *asked*, *if/whether* আৰু বিবৃতিৰ ক্ৰম।
- **Commands/requests:** *told / ordered / requested* + *to* + verb: *"Shut the door," she said to him.* → *She told him to shut the door.* Negative: *not to*.\
- **Exclamations:** *exclaimed with joy/sorrow that …*.

### A. Statements / বিবৃতি

**1.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **He said, "I am tired."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) He said that I was tired.\
(B) He said that he is tired.\
(C) He said that he was tired.\
(D) He said that he had been tired today.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) He said that he was tired.**\
Explanation: Rule: Past reporting verb: 'am' → 'was'; 'I' → 'he'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: অতীত ৰিপ'ৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়া: 'am' → 'was'; 'I' → 'he'।\
*B11-C10-0001 · Statements · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**2.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **She said, "I will come tomorrow."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) She said that she will come tomorrow.\
(B) She said that she would come the next day.\
(C) She said that she would come tomorrow.\
(D) She said that I would come the next day.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) She said that she would come the next day.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Will' → 'would'; 'tomorrow' → 'the next day'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Will' → 'would'; 'tomorrow' → 'the next day'।\
*B11-C10-0002 · Statements · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**3.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **Ram said, "I have finished my work."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Ram said that he had finished his work.\
(B) Ram said that he has finished his work.\
(C) Ram said that he finished my work.\
(D) Ram said that I had finished his work.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) Ram said that he had finished his work.**\
Explanation: Rule: Present perfect → past perfect; pronouns change to match the speaker.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: পুৰা বৰ্তমান → পুৰা অতীত; সৰ্বনাম বক্তা অনুসৰি সলনি হয়।\
*B11-C10-0003 · Statements · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**4.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **The teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) The teacher said that the sun rose in the east.\
(B) The teacher said that the sun had risen in the east.\
(C) The teacher said that the sun was rising in the east.\
(D) The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.**\
Explanation: Rule: A universal truth keeps its present tense.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: চিৰন্তন সত্যৰ কাল সলনি নহয়।\
*B11-C10-0004 · Universal truths · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**5.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **He says, "I am busy."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) He says that he was busy.\
(B) He said that he was busy.\
(C) He says that I am busy.\
(D) He says that he is busy.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) He says that he is busy.**\
Explanation: Rule: The reporting verb 'says' is present, so the tense does not change.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: ৰিপ'ৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়া 'says' বৰ্তমান কালত, সেয়ে কাল সলনি নহয়।\
*B11-C10-0005 · Present reporting verb · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**6.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **She said, "I bought this saree yesterday."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) She said that she bought this saree yesterday.\
(B) She said that she has bought that saree the previous day.\
(C) She said that she had bought this saree yesterday.\
(D) She said that she had bought that saree the previous day.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) She said that she had bought that saree the previous day.**\
Explanation: Rule: Simple past → past perfect; 'this' → 'that'; 'yesterday' → 'the previous day'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: সাধাৰণ অতীত → পুৰা অতীত; 'this' → 'that'; 'yesterday' → 'the previous day'।\
*B11-C10-0006 · Statements · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

### B. Questions, commands and exclamations / প্ৰশ্ন, আদেশ আৰু বিস্ময়

**7.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **He said to me, "Where do you live?"**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) He asked me where I lived.\
(B) He asked me where did I live.\
(C) He told me where I lived.\
(D) He asked me where do you live.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) He asked me where I lived.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Said to' → 'asked'; question becomes statement order; present → past.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Said to' → 'asked'; প্ৰশ্নৰ ক্ৰম বিবৃতিৰ ক্ৰম হয়; বৰ্তমান → অতীত।\
*B11-C10-0007 · Wh-questions · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**8.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **She said to him, "Are you ready?"**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) She asked him are you ready.\
(B) She asked him if he was ready.\
(C) She told him that he was ready.\
(D) She asked him if was he ready.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) She asked him if he was ready.**\
Explanation: Rule: Yes/no questions use 'if' or 'whether' with statement order.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: হয়/নহয় প্ৰশ্নত 'if' বা 'whether' আৰু বিবৃতিৰ ক্ৰম বহে।\
*B11-C10-0008 · Yes/no questions · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**9.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **The teacher said to the students, "Sit down."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) The teacher told the students that sit down.\
(B) The teacher told the students to sit down.\
(C) The teacher said the students to sit down.\
(D) The teacher asked the students if they sat down.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) The teacher told the students to sit down.**\
Explanation: Rule: Commands: told/ordered + object + to + verb.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: আদেশ: told/ordered + কৰ্ম + to + ক্ৰিয়া।\
*B11-C10-0009 · Commands · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**10.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **Mother said to me, "Don't go out in the rain."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Mother told me don't go out in the rain.\
(B) Mother told me to not going out in the rain.\
(C) Mother told me not to go out in the rain.\
(D) Mother said me not to go out in the rain.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) Mother told me not to go out in the rain.**\
Explanation: Rule: Negative command: 'not to' + verb.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: নেতিবাচক আদেশ: 'not to' + ক্ৰিয়া।\
*B11-C10-0010 · Commands · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**11.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **He said to her, "Please lend me your pen."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) He requested her to lend him her pen.\
(B) He requested her to lend me your pen.\
(C) He told her please lend him her pen.\
(D) He requested her that lend him her pen.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) He requested her to lend him her pen.**\
Explanation: Rule: Requests: requested + object + to + verb; pronouns change.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: অনুৰোধ: requested + কৰ্ম + to + ক্ৰিয়া; সৰ্বনাম সলনি হয়।\
*B11-C10-0011 · Requests · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**12.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **She said, "What a beautiful place this is!"**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) She exclaimed what a beautiful place this is.\
(B) She said that what a beautiful place it was.\
(C) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful place.\
(D) She asked that it was a very beautiful place.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful place.**\
Explanation: Rule: Exclamations: 'exclaimed that' + statement with 'very'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: বিস্ময়: 'exclaimed that' + 'very'-ৰে বিবৃতি।\
*B11-C10-0012 · Exclamations · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

**13.** Choose the correct indirect (reported) speech form of: **He said, "Let us go for a walk."**\
তলৰ বাক্যটোৰ শুদ্ধ পৰোক্ষ উক্তি ৰূপটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) He told let us go for a walk.\
(B) He suggested that they should go for a walk.\
(C) He suggested that let them go for a walk.\
(D) He said that we should went for a walk.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) He suggested that they should go for a walk.**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Let us' (a proposal) → 'suggested that … should'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Let us' (প্ৰস্তাৱ) → 'suggested that … should'।\
*B11-C10-0013 · Suggestions · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

(A) The boy said that he can swim.\
(B) The boy said that I could swim.\
(C) The boy said that he could swim.\
(D) The boy said that he could swam.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) The boy said that he could swim.**\

ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Can' → 'could'।\

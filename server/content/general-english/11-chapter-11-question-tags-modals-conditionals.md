# Chapter 11 — Question Tags, Modals and Conditionals / অধ্যায় ১১ — প্ৰশ্ন-টেগ, মডেল আৰু চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ বাক্য

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\
*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 11.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Question tags / প্ৰশ্ন-টেগ:** positive sentence → negative tag; negative sentence → positive tag. Use the same helping verb and a pronoun: *She is a doctor, isn't she? He didn't come, did he?*\
ইতিবাচক বাক্যৰ পিছত নেতিবাচক টেগ, নেতিবাচকৰ পিছত ইতিবাচক টেগ।
- *I am …, aren't I?* · *Let's …, shall we?* · Imperative → *will you?* · *Everyone/nobody …, … they?* · *Nothing …, is it?*\
- Words like *never, hardly, seldom, scarcely, few* make the sentence negative: *He never lies, does he?*

**Modals / মডেল:** *can* (ability), *may* (permission, possibility), *must* (necessity, strong certainty), *should/ought to* (advice, duty), *might* (weak possibility), *need not* (no necessity), *used to* (past habit). After a modal use the base verb (*ought* takes *to*).\
মডেলৰ পিছত মূল ক্ৰিয়া বহে।

**Conditionals / চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ বাক্য**

| Type | If-clause | Main clause | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zero (truth) | present | present | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| First (real) | present | will + V1 | If it rains, we will stay home. |
| Second (unreal present) | past (were) | would + V1 | If I were rich, I would help them. |
| Third (unreal past) | had + V3 | would have + V3 | If he had studied, he would have passed. |

### A. Question tags / প্ৰশ্ন-টেগ

**1.** Choose the correct question tag: **She is a teacher, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) is she\
(B) isn't she\
(C) doesn't she\
(D) wasn't she\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) isn't she**\
Explanation: Rule: Positive sentence with 'is' → negative tag 'isn't she'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'is' থকা ইতিবাচক বাক্য → নেতিবাচক টেগ 'isn't she'।\
*B11-C11-0001 · Question tags · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**2.** Choose the correct question tag: **They didn't come, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) did they\
(B) didn't they\
(C) do they\
(D) were they\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) did they**\
Explanation: Rule: Negative sentence → positive tag with 'did'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: নেতিবাচক বাক্য → 'did'-ৰে ইতিবাচক টেগ।\
*B11-C11-0002 · Question tags · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**3.** Choose the correct question tag: **I am right, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) aren't I\
(B) am not I\
(C) isn't I\
(D) don't I\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) aren't I**\
Explanation: Rule: The standard tag for 'I am' is 'aren't I'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'I am'-ৰ শুদ্ধ টেগ 'aren't I'।\
*B11-C11-0003 · Question tags · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**4.** Choose the correct question tag: **Let's go to the market, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) will we\
(B) don't we\
(C) shall we\
(D) let we\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) shall we**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Let's' takes the tag 'shall we'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Let's'-ৰ টেগ 'shall we'।\
*B11-C11-0004 · Question tags · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**5.** Choose the correct question tag: **Close the door, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) do you\
(B) shall we\
(C) will you\
(D) don't you\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) will you**\
Explanation: Rule: An imperative usually takes 'will you'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: আদেশসূচক বাক্যত সাধাৰণতে 'will you' টেগ বহে।\
*B11-C11-0005 · Question tags · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**6.** Choose the correct question tag: **He never tells a lie, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) doesn't he\
(B) is he\
(C) did he\
(D) does he\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) does he**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Never' makes the sentence negative, so the tag is positive.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Never'-এ বাক্যটো নেতিবাচক কৰে, সেয়ে টেগ ইতিবাচক।\
*B11-C11-0006 · Question tags · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

**7.** Choose the correct question tag: **Everyone was present, ___?**\
শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন-টেগটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) wasn't he\
(B) was they\
(C) weren't everyone\
(D) weren't they\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) weren't they**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Everyone', 'somebody', 'nobody' take 'they' in the tag.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Everyone', 'somebody', 'nobody'-ৰ টেগত 'they' বহে।\

### B. Modals / মডেল

**8.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **You ___ respect your elders.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) can\
(B) should\
(C) may\
(D) might\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) should**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Should' expresses duty or advice.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: কৰ্তব্য বা পৰামৰ্শ বুজাবলৈ 'should'।\

(A) Must\
(B) Should\
(C) Need\
(D) May\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) May**\
Explanation: Rule: 'May' is used to ask for permission politely.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: ভদ্ৰভাৱে অনুমতি বিচাৰিবলৈ 'May'।\

খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) need\
(B) must\
(C) should\
(D) would\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) need**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Need not' means it is not necessary.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Need not' মানে প্ৰয়োজন নাই।\

(A) uses\
(B) used\
(C) was used\
(D) use\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) used**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Used to' expresses a past habit.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: অতীতৰ অভ্যাস বুজাবলৈ 'used to'।\

**12.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **We ought ___ help the poor.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) to\
(B) for\
(C) that\
(D) be\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) to**\
Explanation: Rule: 'Ought' is always followed by 'to'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'Ought'-ৰ পিছত সদায় 'to' বহে।\

### C. Conditionals / চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ বাক্য

**13.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **If it rains, we ___ at home.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) would stay\
(B) will stay\
(C) stayed\
(D) would have stayed\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) will stay**\
Explanation: Rule: First conditional: if + present, will + base verb.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: প্ৰথম চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ: if + বৰ্তমান, will + মূল ক্ৰিয়া।\
*B11-C11-0013 · Conditionals · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**14.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **If I ___ a bird, I would fly.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) am\
(B) was being\
(C) were\
(D) had been\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) were**\
Explanation: Rule: Second conditional (imaginary): 'if I were'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: দ্বিতীয় চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ (কাল্পনিক): 'if I were'।\
*B11-C11-0014 · Conditionals · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**15.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **If he had worked hard, he ___ passed.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) will have\
(B) would\
(C) would have\
(D) had\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) would have**\
Explanation: Rule: Third conditional: if + had V3, would have + V3.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: তৃতীয় চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ: if + had V3, would have + V3।\
*B11-C11-0015 · Conditionals · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**16.** Choose the correct option to fill in the blank: **If you heat water to 100 °C, it ___.**\
খালী ঠাইত বহুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) will boiled\
(B) would boil\
(C) boils\
(D) boiled\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) boils**\
Explanation: Rule: Zero conditional for scientific facts: present + present.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: বৈজ্ঞানিক সত্যৰ বাবে শূন্য চৰ্তসাপেক্ষ: বৰ্তমান + বৰ্তমান।\
*B11-C11-0016 · Conditionals · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**17.** Choose the grammatically correct sentence:\
ব্যাকৰণগতভাৱে শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) If I would have known, I would have helped you.\
(B) If I knew, I would have help you.\
(C) If I had knew, I would helped you.\
(D) If I had known, I would have helped you.\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) If I had known, I would have helped you.**\
Explanation: Rule: Never use 'would' in the if-clause of a third conditional.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: তৃতীয় চৰ্তসাপেক্ষৰ if-উপবাক্যত 'would' নবহে।\
*B11-C11-0017 · Conditionals · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

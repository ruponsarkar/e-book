# Chapter 12 — Spot the Error / অধ্যায় ১২ — ভুল বাছক

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\
*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 12.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**How to solve / কেনেকৈ সমাধান কৰিব:** read the whole sentence first; then check, in this order: (1) subject–verb agreement, (2) tense, (3) pronoun, (4) article and preposition, (5) adjective/adverb form, (6) word order and extra words.\
প্ৰথমে গোটেই বাক্যটো পঢ়ক; তাৰ পিছত ক্ৰমে কৰ্তা–ক্ৰিয়াৰ সংগতি, কাল, সৰ্বনাম, আৰ্টিকেল আৰু প্ৰিপজিচন, বিশেষণ/ক্ৰিয়া-বিশেষণ, শব্দৰ ক্ৰম পৰীক্ষা কৰক।

**Errors examiners use most / পৰীক্ষকে বেছিকৈ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা ভুল**

| Error / ভুল | Wrong / অশুদ্ধ | Right / শুদ্ধ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present perfect with past time | I have seen him yesterday. | I saw him yesterday. |
| Double comparative | more better | better |
| *Each/one* + plural | Each of the boys have | Each of the boys has |
| *Since/for* | for 2020 | since 2020 |
| *Return back, repeat again* (redundancy) | He returned back. | He returned. |
| Uncountable nouns made plural | informations, furnitures, sceneries | information, furniture, scenery |
| *Did* + past form | He did not went. | He did not go. |
| *Lest … not* | lest he should not fall | lest he should fall |
| Object pronoun after preposition | between you and I | between you and me |
| *Discuss about, enter into (a room)* | discuss about the plan | discuss the plan |

**Option (D) "No error"** is correct only when every part is right — check it last, not first.\
**বিকল্প (D) "No error"** কেৱল তেতিয়াহে শুদ্ধ, যেতিয়া সকলো অংশ শুদ্ধ।

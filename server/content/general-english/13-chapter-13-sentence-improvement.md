# Chapter 13 — Sentence Improvement and Correction / অধ্যায় ১৩ — বাক্য শুধৰণি

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\

## 13.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Method / পদ্ধতি:** read the sentence with each option in place of the italic part. The right option fixes the error **without changing the meaning**. Choose "No improvement" only when the italic part is already correct.\
হেলনীয়া অংশৰ ঠাইত প্ৰতিটো বিকল্প বহুৱাই পঢ়ক। শুদ্ধ বিকল্পই অৰ্থ সলনি নকৰাকৈ ভুলটো শুধৰায়।

**Common patterns / সাধাৰণ ধৰণ**
- *It is high time we* **left** (past form after *it is high time*).\
*It is high time*-ৰ পিছত অতীত ৰূপ।
- *I wish I* **were** rich.\
- *One should do* **one's** duty.\
- *Scarcely had he arrived* **when** …\
- *He is* **one of the best players** (plural noun after *one of the*).\
- *The reason is* **that** … (not *because*).\
- *Would you mind* **opening** *the window?* (-ing after *mind*).\
- *He is used to* **working** *late* (-ing after *be used to*).

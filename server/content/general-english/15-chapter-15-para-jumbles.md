# Chapter 15 — Para Jumbles and Sentence Arrangement / অধ্যায় ১৫ — বাক্য সজোৱা

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\
*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 15.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Method / পদ্ধতি**
1. Find the **opening part**: it usually has the subject and does not start with a linking word or pronoun (*he, it, this, but, so*).\
**আৰম্ভণিৰ অংশ** বিচাৰক: সাধাৰণতে কৰ্তা থাকে, সৰ্বনাম বা সংযোজক শব্দেৰে আৰম্ভ নহয়।
2. Find **mandatory pairs**: a noun and the pronoun that refers to it; *not only … but also*; a question and its answer; a cause and its result.\
**অনিবাৰ্য যোৰ** বিচাৰক: বিশেষ্য আৰু তাৰ সৰ্বনাম; কাৰণ আৰু ফল।
3. Look for **time order** (*first, then, finally*) and **general → specific** order.\
**সময়ৰ ক্ৰম** আৰু **সাধাৰণৰ পৰা নিৰ্দিষ্টলৈ** ক্ৰম চাওক।
4. Use the options: eliminate any option that starts with an impossible part.\
বিকল্পবোৰ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক: অসম্ভৱ অংশেৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা বিকল্প বাদ দিয়ক।

### A. Sentence parts (P, Q, R, S) / বাক্যৰ অংশ

**1.** Arrange the parts P, Q, R and S to form a meaningful sentence:\
P, Q, R আৰু S অংশকেইটা সজাই এটা অৰ্থপূৰ্ণ বাক্য গঠন কৰা:\
P: to the market\
Q: my mother went\
R: to buy vegetables\
S: yesterday morning\
(A) PQRS\
(B) SRQP\
(C) QPRS\
(D) RQPS\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) QPRS**\
Explanation: Rule: 'My mother went (Q) to the market (P) to buy vegetables (R) yesterday morning (S).'\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: 'My mother went to the market to buy vegetables yesterday morning.'\

### B. Sentences in a paragraph / অনুচ্ছেদৰ বাক্য

**9.** The first (1) and last (6) sentences are given. Arrange P, Q, R and S in the correct order:\
প্ৰথম (১) আৰু শেষ (৬) বাক্য দিয়া আছে। P, Q, R আৰু S শুদ্ধ ক্ৰমত সজোৱা:\
1. Majuli is a river island in the Brahmaputra.\
P: These sattras were set up after the time of Sankardeva.\
Q: It is known as the centre of Assamese Vaishnavite culture.\
R: Today they still keep alive the traditions of dance, music and mask-making.\
S: The island has many sattras, or Vaishnavite monasteries.\
6. Thousands of visitors come to see the Raas festival every year.\
(A) QSPR\
(B) PSQR\
(C) QPSR\
(D) SPQR\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) QSPR**\
Explanation: Rule: Q ('It' = Majuli) follows sentence 1; S introduces 'sattras'; P ('These sattras') needs S before it; R ('they') follows P.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: Q-ৰ 'It' = Majuli; S-এ 'sattras'-ৰ কথা কয়; P-ৰ 'These sattras'-ৰ আগত S লাগে; R-ৰ 'they' P-ৰ পিছত আহে।\
*B11-C15-0009 · Paragraph order · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

**10.** The first (1) and last (6) sentences are given. Arrange P, Q, R and S in the correct order:\
প্ৰথম (১) আৰু শেষ (৬) বাক্য দিয়া আছে। P, Q, R আৰু S শুদ্ধ ক্ৰমত সজোৱা:\
1. Ramesh wanted to become a police officer.\
P: So he began to study and exercise every day.\
Q: He knew that the test needed both knowledge and fitness.\
R: After a year of hard work, he sat for the examination.\
S: He passed it in his first attempt.\
6. His parents were very proud of him.\
(A) PQRS\
(B) QRPS\
(C) QPRS\
(D) RQPS\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) QPRS**\
Explanation: Rule: Q gives the reason; P ('So') is the result; R and S follow in time order.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: Q-এ কাৰণ দিয়ে; P ('So') ফল; R আৰু S সময়ৰ ক্ৰমত আহে।\
*B11-C15-0010 · Paragraph order · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**11.** The first (1) and last (6) sentences are given. Arrange P, Q, R and S in the correct order:\
প্ৰথম (১) আৰু শেষ (৬) বাক্য দিয়া আছে। P, Q, R আৰু S শুদ্ধ ক্ৰমত সজোৱা:\
1. Tea is the most important industry of Assam.\
P: Robert Bruce found tea plants growing wild here in 1823.\
Q: Today Assam produces about half of India's tea.\
R: Its history goes back two hundred years.\
S: Soon the British set up the first gardens.\
6. Assam tea is famous all over the world for its strong taste.\
(A) PRSQ\
(B) RSPQ\
(C) QRPS\
(D) RPSQ\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) RPSQ**\
Explanation: Rule: R introduces 'its history'; P is the start (1823); S follows ('Soon'); Q ('Today') brings it to the present.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: R-এ ইতিহাসৰ কথা আৰম্ভ কৰে; P আৰম্ভণি (১৮২৩); S ('Soon') পিছত; Q ('Today') বৰ্তমানলৈ আনে।\
*B11-C15-0011 · Paragraph order · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**12.** The first (1) and last (6) sentences are given. Arrange P, Q, R and S in the correct order:\
প্ৰথম (১) আৰু শেষ (৬) বাক্য দিয়া আছে। P, Q, R আৰু S শুদ্ধ ক্ৰমত সজোৱা:\

P: Yet in many places people do not get clean water.\
Q: Plants, animals and human beings all need it.\

**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) QPSR**\

ব্যাখ্যা: নিয়ম: Q-এ ১ নং বাক্য বুজায়; P ('Yet') বৈপৰীত্য; S সমস্যা; R ('therefore') সিদ্ধান্ত।\

*Level: Grade IV to Graduate · Questions and options in English as in the exam; rules and explanations in English and Assamese*\

## 17.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Method / পদ্ধতি:** first find the meaning of the word, then the option opposite to it. Watch for **synonym traps** — an option with the same meaning is usually included.\
প্ৰথমে শব্দটোৰ অৰ্থ বিচাৰক, তাৰ পিছত বিপৰীত অৰ্থৰ বিকল্প। একে অৰ্থৰ বিকল্পৰ ফান্দৰ পৰা সাৱধান।

**Prefixes that make antonyms / বিপৰীত অৰ্থ দিয়া উপসৰ্গ:** *un-* (happy – unhappy), *in-/im-/il-/ir-* (possible – impossible, legal – illegal, regular – irregular), *dis-* (agree – disagree), *mis-* (understand – misunderstand), *non-* (violent – nonviolent).

| Word | Antonym |
| --- | --- |
| ancient | modern |
| artificial | natural |
| expand | contract |
| humble | proud |
| optimist | pessimist |
| permanent | temporary |
| scarcity | abundance |
| transparent | opaque |

**1.** Choose the word opposite in meaning (antonym) to: **Ancient**\
তলৰ শব্দটোৰ বিপৰীত অৰ্থৰ শব্দটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) Old\
(B) Historic\
(C) Ruined\
(D) Modern\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Modern**\
Explanation: 'Ancient' means very old; its opposite is 'modern'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: 'Ancient' মানে অতি পুৰণি; বিপৰীত 'modern' (আধুনিক)।\

## 20.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Rules that solve most spelling questions / বেছিভাগ বানানৰ প্ৰশ্ন সমাধান কৰা নিয়ম**
- **i before e except after c** (when the sound is 'ee'): *believe, achieve, receive, ceiling*. Exceptions: *seize, weird, either, neither*.\
'ee' ধ্বনিত c-ৰ পিছত নহ'লে i আগত, e পিছত।
- **Doubling:** a one-syllable word ending consonant–vowel–consonant doubles the last letter: *run – running, stop – stopped*. In longer words, double when the stress is on the last syllable: *begin – beginning, occur – occurred* (but *visit – visited*).\
- **Silent e** drops before a vowel suffix: *write – writing, hope – hoping*; it stays before a consonant suffix: *hopeful, movement*.\
- **-y after a consonant → -i:** *happy – happiness, carry – carried*.

**Commonly misspelt words / সাধাৰণতে ভুল বানান হোৱা শব্দ:** accommodate, achievement, address, committee, conscience, definitely, embarrass, environment, exaggerate, government, guarantee, independent, maintenance, millennium, necessary, occasion, occurrence, privilege, pronunciation, receive, recommend, rhythm, separate, successful, tomorrow.

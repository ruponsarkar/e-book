## 22.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Method / পদ্ধতি**
1. Read the whole passage once without filling anything, to get the topic and tone.\
প্ৰথমে একো নভৰাকৈ গোটেই অনুচ্ছেদটো পঢ়ক।
2. For each blank, decide the part of speech needed (noun, verb, preposition, connector).\
প্ৰতিটো খালী ঠাইত কোন শব্দ-শ্ৰেণী লাগে ঠিক কৰক।
3. Check grammar (tense, agreement, article) and collocation.\
4. Re-read the full passage with your answers.\
উত্তৰসহ গোটেই অনুচ্ছেদটো আকৌ পঢ়ক।

Each question below repeats the sentence with its blank, so it can also be practised on its own.\
তলৰ প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্নত খালী ঠাইযুক্ত বাক্যটো পুনৰ দিয়া হৈছে, যাতে অকলেও অনুশীলন কৰিব পাৰি।

### Passage 1 — Tea in Assam / অনুচ্ছেদ ১ — অসমৰ চাহ

> Assam is famous **(1)** ___ its tea. The tea plant grows well in the warm and **(2)** ___ climate of the Brahmaputra valley. Workers pluck two leaves and a bud **(3)** ___ hand. The leaves are then **(4)** ___ to the factory, where they are dried and processed. Assam tea is known for its strong taste, **(5)** ___ it is popular all over the world.

**1.** Choose the word that best fits the blank in the passage: **(1) Assam is famous ___ its tea.**\
অনুচ্ছেদটোৰ খালী ঠাইত আটাইতকৈ উপযুক্ত শব্দটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) of\
(B) for\
(C) with\
(D) by\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) for**\
Explanation: The fixed expression is 'famous for'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: নিৰ্দিষ্ট প্ৰয়োগ 'famous for'।\

অনুচ্ছেদটোৰ খালী ঠাইত আটাইতকৈ উপযুক্ত শব্দটো বাছি উলিওৱা:\
(A) humid\
(B) dry\
(C) frozen\
(D) cold\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) humid**\
Explanation: Tea needs warm, wet conditions; 'humid' fits with 'warm'.\

### Passage 2 — Floods / অনুচ্ছেদ ২ — বান

(B) effect\
(C) affects\
(D) affected by\

Explanation: Plural subject 'floods' and a general fact: 'affect'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: বহুবচন কৰ্তা 'floods' আৰু সাধাৰণ সত্য: 'affect'।\

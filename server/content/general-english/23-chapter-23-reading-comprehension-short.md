# Chapter 23 — Reading Comprehension: Short Passages / অধ্যায় ২৩ — পঠন-বোধগম্যতা: চমু অনুচ্ছেদ

*প্ৰশ্ন আৰু বিকল্প পৰীক্ষাৰ দৰে ইংৰাজীত; নিয়ম আৰু ব্যাখ্যা ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত*

## 23.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Method / পদ্ধতি**
1. Read the questions first, then the passage — you will know what to look for.\
প্ৰথমে প্ৰশ্নবোৰ পঢ়ক, তাৰ পিছত অনুচ্ছেদ।
2. **Fact questions:** find the exact line in the passage. **Main idea / title questions:** choose the option that covers the whole passage, not one detail.\
**তথ্যৰ প্ৰশ্ন:** অনুচ্ছেদৰ নিৰ্দিষ্ট শাৰী বিচাৰক। **মূল ভাৱ/শিৰোনাম:** গোটেই অনুচ্ছেদ সামৰা বিকল্প বাছক।
3. **Word-meaning questions:** read the sentence around the word; the meaning in context decides.\
**শব্দৰ অৰ্থ:** শব্দটোৰ চাৰিওফালৰ বাক্য পঢ়ক।
4. Do not use outside knowledge if the passage says something different.\
অনুচ্ছেদে কোৱা কথাৰ বাহিৰৰ জ্ঞান ব্যৱহাৰ নকৰিব।

The passages in this chapter were written for this book. Questions refer to the passage just above them, so this chapter is not used in the mixed test papers.\
এই অধ্যায়ৰ অনুচ্ছেদবোৰ এই কিতাপৰ বাবে লিখা। প্ৰশ্নবোৰ ঠিক ওপৰৰ অনুচ্ছেদৰ ওপৰত আধাৰিত, সেয়ে এই অধ্যায় মিশ্ৰ আৰ্হি প্ৰশ্নকাকতত ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা হোৱা নাই।

### Passage 1 — The village library / অনুচ্ছেদ ১ — গাঁৱৰ পুথিভঁৰাল

> Ten years ago, the village of Kumargaon had no library. Children who wanted to read story books had to travel twelve kilometres to the town. Then a retired schoolteacher, Mr Bora, gave two rooms of his house and three hundred of his own books to start a small library. At first only a few children came. Slowly the number grew, and the villagers began to donate books and money. Today the library has more than four thousand books and a reading room that stays open every evening. Mr Bora says the greatest reward is seeing children who once could not read now borrowing books every week.

**1.** Before the library opened, children had to:\
(A) Buy books from Mr Bora\
(B) Travel twelve kilometres to read story books\
(C) Read only school textbooks at home\
(D) Wait for a mobile library\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) Travel twelve kilometres to read story books**\
Explanation: The passage says children 'had to travel twelve kilometres to the town'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদত কোৱা হৈছে যে শিশুসকলে বাৰ কিলোমিটাৰ যাব লগা হৈছিল।\
*B11-C23-0001 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**2.** Mr Bora started the library by giving:\
(A) Money to the village\
(B) Four thousand books\
(C) Land for a new building\
(D) Two rooms and three hundred books\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Two rooms and three hundred books**\
Explanation: He gave 'two rooms of his house and three hundred of his own books'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: তেওঁ ঘৰৰ দুটা কোঠা আৰু নিজৰ তিনিশ কিতাপ দিছিল।\
*B11-C23-0002 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**3.** The word 'donate' in the passage means:\
(A) Sell\
(B) Give as a gift\
(C) Borrow\
(D) Lend for a short time\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) Give as a gift**\
Explanation: Villagers gave books and money freely to help; 'donate' means give as a gift.\
ব্যাখ্যা: গাঁওবাসীয়ে বিনামূলীয়াকৈ দিছিল; 'donate' মানে দান কৰা।\
*B11-C23-0003 · Word meaning · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**4.** What does Mr Bora consider his greatest reward?\
(A) The money the villagers gave him\
(B) The large size of the reading room\
(C) Praise from the town\
(D) Seeing children who could not read now borrowing books\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Seeing children who could not read now borrowing books**\
Explanation: The last sentence states this directly.\
ব্যাখ্যা: শেষৰ বাক্যত এই কথা পোনপটীয়াকৈ কোৱা আছে।\
*B11-C23-0004 · Fact questions · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**5.** The best title for the passage is:\
(A) The History of Kumargaon\
(B) How to Build a House\
(C) A Teacher's Gift to His Village\
(D) Children Who Do Not Read\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) A Teacher's Gift to His Village**\
Explanation: The passage is about how Mr Bora's gift became the village library.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদটো মিঃ বৰাৰ দানৰ পৰা গাঁৱৰ পুথিভঁৰাল গঢ়ি উঠাৰ বিষয়ে।\
*B11-C23-0005 · Title · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

### Passage 2 — The one-horned rhino / অনুচ্ছেদ ২ — এখৰ্গীয়া গঁড়

> The greater one-horned rhinoceros is found mainly in the grasslands of Assam and Nepal. It is a large animal with thick, folded skin that looks like armour. Although it appears slow, it can run fast when it is in danger. In the early twentieth century, hunting reduced its numbers to a very small population. Strict protection in parks such as Kaziranga has helped it recover. However, poaching for its horn and the loss of grassland to floods and settlements are still serious threats.

**6.** According to the passage, the rhino's skin looks like:\
(A) Grass\
(B) Rock\
(C) Armour\
(D) Leather bags\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) Armour**\
Explanation: The passage says its skin 'looks like armour'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদত কোৱা হৈছে যে ইয়াৰ ছাল বৰ্মৰ দৰে দেখি।\
*B11-C23-0006 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**7.** Why did the number of rhinos fall in the early twentieth century?\
(A) Because of hunting\
(B) Because of floods only\
(C) Because of a disease\
(D) Because they moved to Nepal\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) Because of hunting**\
Explanation: 'Hunting reduced its numbers' is stated in the passage.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদত চিকাৰৰ বাবে সংখ্যা কমি অহাৰ কথা কোৱা হৈছে।\
*B11-C23-0007 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**8.** Which of these is NOT mentioned as a present threat?\
(A) Poaching for its horn\
(B) Loss of grassland to floods\
(C) Loss of grassland to settlements\
(D) Climate in the mountains\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (D) Climate in the mountains**\
Explanation: The passage names poaching, floods and settlements, but not mountain climate.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদত চোৰাংচিকাৰ, বান আৰু বসতিৰ কথা আছে, পাহাৰৰ জলবায়ুৰ কথা নাই।\
*B11-C23-0008 · Negative questions · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**9.** The word 'recover' in the passage means:\
(A) Hide from people\
(B) Become extinct\
(C) Return to a healthy number\
(D) Move to another place\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) Return to a healthy number**\
Explanation: After protection, the population grew again: 'recover' means return to a good state.\
ব্যাখ্যা: সুৰক্ষাৰ পিছত সংখ্যা আকৌ বাঢ়িল; 'recover' মানে পূৰ্বৰ অৱস্থালৈ ঘূৰি অহা।\
*B11-C23-0009 · Word meaning · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**10.** What is the main idea of the passage?\
(A) Rhinos are the fastest animals in Assam\
(B) The rhino has recovered with protection but still faces threats\
(C) Kaziranga is the largest park in India\
(D) Hunting of rhinos is allowed in Nepal\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) The rhino has recovered with protection but still faces threats**\
Explanation: The passage describes the rhino, its decline, recovery and continuing threats.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদত গঁড়ৰ বৰ্ণনা, সংখ্যা হ্ৰাস, পুনৰুদ্ধাৰ আৰু এতিয়াও থকা বিপদৰ কথা আছে।\
*B11-C23-0010 · Main idea · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

### Passage 3 — Saving electricity / অনুচ্ছেদ ৩ — বিদ্যুৎ সঞ্চয়

> Electricity is produced at a cost, and much of it in India still comes from burning coal. Every unit we waste adds to this cost and to air pollution. Simple habits can make a big difference. Switching off lights and fans when leaving a room, using LED bulbs and not leaving chargers plugged in all save power. If every household saved just one unit a day, the country would need fewer new power plants.

**11.** According to the passage, wasting electricity increases:\
(A) Cost and air pollution\
(B) The number of LED bulbs\
(C) Rainfall\
(D) The price of fans\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) Cost and air pollution**\
Explanation: 'Every unit we waste adds to this cost and to air pollution.'\
ব্যাখ্যা: অপচয় কৰা প্ৰতিটো ইউনিটে খৰচ আৰু বায়ু প্ৰদূষণ বঢ়ায়।\
*B11-C23-0011 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**12.** Which habit is mentioned for saving power?\
(A) Buying more fans\
(B) Keeping lights on at night\
(C) Using LED bulbs\
(D) Charging phones all day\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (C) Using LED bulbs**\
Explanation: The passage lists switching off lights and fans, using LED bulbs and unplugging chargers.\
ব্যাখ্যা: অনুচ্ছেদত LED বাল্ব ব্যৱহাৰৰ কথা কোৱা আছে।\
*B11-C23-0012 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*

**13.** What would happen if every household saved one unit a day?\
(A) The country would need fewer new power plants\
(B) Electricity would become free\
(C) Coal would no longer be used\
(D) Every house would get LED bulbs\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) The country would need fewer new power plants**\
Explanation: The last sentence says the country would need fewer new power plants.\
ব্যাখ্যা: শেষৰ বাক্যত কম নতুন বিদ্যুৎ কেন্দ্ৰৰ প্ৰয়োজন হ'ব বুলি কোৱা হৈছে।\
*B11-C23-0013 · Inference · Moderate · ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, ADRE-III-P5, APC, APSI, SSC*

**14.** The tone of the passage is:\
(A) Angry\
(B) Advisory\
(C) Humorous\
(D) Sad\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) Advisory**\
Explanation: The writer advises readers to adopt simple habits.\
ব্যাখ্যা: লেখকে পাঠকক সৰল অভ্যাস গ্ৰহণ কৰিবলৈ পৰামৰ্শ দিছে।\
*B11-C23-0014 · Tone · Hard · ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P4, APSI, SSC*

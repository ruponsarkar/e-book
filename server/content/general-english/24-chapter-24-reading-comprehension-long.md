## 24.1 Rules and revision notes / নিয়ম আৰু পুনৰালোচনাৰ টোকা

**Paper 4 strategy / প্ৰশ্নকাকত ৪ৰ কৌশল:** Reading Comprehension and English Language questions carry 2 marks each, so they are worth twice a General Studies question. Give about 2 minutes per question.\
প্ৰশ্নকাকত ৪ত পঠন-বোধগম্যতাৰ প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্নৰ মূল্য ২ নম্বৰ; প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্নত প্ৰায় ২ মিনিট দিয়ক।

**Question types / প্ৰশ্নৰ প্ৰকাৰ**
- **Inference:** what the author suggests but does not say directly. Choose the option the passage *supports*, not one that is merely possible.\
**অনুমান:** লেখকে পোনপটীয়াকৈ নকৈ ইংগিত দিয়া কথা।
- **Author's view / tone:** look for judging words (*unfortunately, encouraging, must*).\
**লেখকৰ মত/সুৰ:** মূল্যায়ন কৰা শব্দ বিচাৰক।

### Passage 1 — Digital payments in rural Assam / অনুচ্ছেদ ১ — গ্ৰাম্য অসমত ডিজিটেল পৰিশোধ

> A decade ago, a vegetable seller in a weekly haat in rural Assam would have accepted only cash. Today many such sellers display a QR code on a small board beside their baskets. The change has been driven by cheap smartphones, low-cost mobile data and the spread of instant payment systems that move money between bank accounts in seconds.
>
> The benefits are real. Sellers no longer need to carry large amounts of cash home after dark, and buyers do not have to worry about change. Small sums received through the day also build a record of income, which can later help a seller obtain a bank loan without collateral.
>
> Yet the transition is uneven. Older traders, especially women who have had little schooling, often depend on a relative to check whether a payment has arrived. Weak network coverage in hilly and char areas can make a transaction fail at the very moment a customer wants to pay. Fraudsters, too, have learned to exploit the new system, sending fake payment screenshots or tricking users into sharing their PIN.
>
> Digital payments, therefore, are not simply a matter of technology. Their success depends on reliable networks, on patient training that builds confidence, and on a culture of caution that teaches every user never to share a PIN or a one-time password.

**1.** According to the passage, which of these has NOT driven the change to digital payments?\
(A) Cheap smartphones\
(B) Government orders banning cash at haats\
(C) Low-cost mobile data\
(D) Instant payment systems\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (B) Government orders banning cash at haats**\
Explanation: The first paragraph names smartphones, data and instant payments; no ban on cash is mentioned.\
ব্যাখ্যা: প্ৰথম অনুচ্ছেদত স্মাৰ্টফ'ন, ডাটা আৰু তৎক্ষণাৎ পৰিশোধৰ কথা আছে; নগদ ধন নিষিদ্ধ কৰাৰ কথা নাই।\

### Passage 2 — Wetlands / অনুচ্ছেদ ২ — জলাশয়

> For a long time, wetlands were regarded as wasteland — marshy ground that bred mosquitoes and could be put to better use only if it was drained and filled. Across many Indian cities, beels and ponds were quietly converted into housing plots and roads.
>
> Scientists now describe wetlands very differently. A healthy wetland acts like a sponge: it soaks up excess rainwater and releases it slowly, reducing urban flooding. It filters pollutants from the water that passes through it, supports fish that are a source of food and income, and provides a resting place for migratory birds that travel thousands of kilometres every winter.
>
> The cost of ignoring these services is visible after every heavy downpour, when streets in low-lying areas turn into rivers. Engineers can build drains, but drains carry water away; they do not store it. Protecting the remaining wetlands, and restoring the ones that have been damaged, is therefore one of the cheapest ways a city can prepare for a future of more intense rainfall.

**8.** In the past, wetlands were mainly seen as:\
(A) Wasteland to be drained and filled\
(B) Important places for birds\
(C) Natural flood barriers\
(D) Sources of drinking water\
**Answer / উত্তৰ: (A) Wasteland to be drained and filled**\
Explanation: The first sentence says they were 'regarded as wasteland'.\
ব্যাখ্যা: প্ৰথম বাক্যত সিহঁতক পতিত ভূমি বুলি গণ্য কৰাৰ কথা কোৱা হৈছে।\
*B11-C24-0008 · Fact questions · Easy · ADRE-IV-P1, ADRE-IV-P2, ADRE-III-P3, ADRE-III-P5, APC, SSC*
